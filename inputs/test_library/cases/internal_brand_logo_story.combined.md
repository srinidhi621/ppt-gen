## Ascendion Brand Identity
- Position the corporate logo prominently with clear whitespace.
- Use one supporting motif from the dimensional keyword artwork family.
- Keep the narrative focused on innovation, reliability, and velocity.

---

<!-- section_id: brand_logo_guidance -->
## Logo Usage Guardrails
- Use official black or white primary logo variants only.
- Preserve aspect ratio and avoid stretching.
- Pair logo with a simple supporting phrase, not dense text.

---

<!-- section_id: brand_close -->
## Closing Brand Moment
- Use the tribal mark as a high-contrast visual anchor.
- End with one short statement about transformation outcomes.
